Rules the solver enforces
  • Hard (never relaxed): certificate match · on site now · not already moving · not at a post that is itself short · rested (under 3 h without a break) · source post keeps its minimum cover · no double-booking across gaps.
  • Soft (minimised, in order of weight): pulling someone off a post costs 30 vs 10 from standby · walk time ×2 per minute · hours already worked ×3 · stated preferences ±.
  • All open gaps are solved together by exact branch-and-bound search over the feasible assignments. If no full plan exists, the best partial plan is shown with the reason for each unfilled slot.
